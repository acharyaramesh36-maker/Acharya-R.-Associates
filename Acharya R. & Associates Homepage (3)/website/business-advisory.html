Business Advisory

Sound financial advice for important decisions.

We help owners and management teams think through decisions about growth, structure, funding and performance, using financial analysis grounded in the realities of doing business in Nepal.

Why advisory support

Decisions about expansion, investment or restructuring are easier to make with a clear view of the numbers and their consequences.

We prepare analysis, projections and plans that set out options and their financial effects, and we discuss them with you until the decision and its risks are clear.

Planning a change?

Let’s talk through your next decision.

Tell us what you are considering and the questions you need answered. We will propose how we can help.

Our approach

Analysis first, advice second.

We begin with your objectives and the decision in front of you, then gather the financial and operating information needed to assess it.

We build projections, scenarios and comparisons that show the likely effect of each option on profit, cash flow and funding.

We present our findings in plain terms, discuss the assumptions behind them and help you plan how to implement the chosen course.
