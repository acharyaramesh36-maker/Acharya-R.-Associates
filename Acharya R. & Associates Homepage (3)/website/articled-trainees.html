Overview

Practical training that builds professional judgement.

Articleship is where professional study meets practice. As an articled trainee you work on live engagements, learn how standards are applied and see how different businesses operate.

Training is undertaken in line with the articleship requirements of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of Nepal. Your responsibilities grow as your experience and confidence develop.
