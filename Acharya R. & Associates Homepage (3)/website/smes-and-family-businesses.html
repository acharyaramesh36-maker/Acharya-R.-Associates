SMEs & Family Businesses

Small and medium enterprises and family-owned businesses often run with lean finance teams and owners who wear many hats. We help them keep proper books, meet tax and statutory obligations, and build the financial information and governance they need to grow and to pass the business on.

Industry
perspective

SMEs & Family Businesses

In an owner-managed business, good financial information frees the owner to focus on running the business.

Many SMEs keep records primarily for tax, which leaves owners without timely information on profitability, cash and receivables.

As the business grows, lenders, partners and new family members expect formal accounts, clear policies and separation between business and personal finances.

Succession and ownership changes raise questions about valuation, structure and governance that are best addressed well in advance.

Our approach

Practical support for owner-managed businesses.

We take time to understand the business, the family or owners behind it and their plans for the future.

We can maintain your books, prepare tax filings and financial statements, or support your in-house team with reviews and year-end work.

When larger decisions arise, from bank financing to restructuring or succession, we provide analysis and advice in plain language.
